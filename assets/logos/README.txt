Tips:
- Use each company's official logo (download from their brand/press/media kit
  or a logo library you have rights to use).
- The logos sit on white "chips", so full-colour logos on a transparent or
  white background look best. Trim excess whitespace for even sizing.
- Recommended height ~60px (the CSS caps display height at 30px and scales down).
- Until a file is present, that slot automatically shows the company NAME as
  styled text, so the page never shows a broken image.

Note: company logos are trademarks. Only display them if you have permission /
an actual relationship, or change the heading to neutral wording like
"Roles our members have targeted" to keep the claim accurate.
